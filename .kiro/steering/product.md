# Product: Ghosts of Here

A narrated ghost tour of any place on Earth, built from open data about what's gone: demolished buildings (Wikidata), their stories (Wikipedia) and wildlife no longer recorded (GBIF). Kiroween 2026, **Resurrection** category.

## Who it's for
- Curious locals and tourists who want to know what stood here before.
- History and geography teachers looking for a hook for a field trip.

## Pillars
1. **Anywhere, instantly.** Any address or "use my location"; no account, no install.
2. **Real records only.** Every ghost links to its Wikidata item, Wikipedia article or GBIF species page. Never invent history.
3. **Honest uncertainty.** "No longer recorded" is not "extinct". Say what the data can and can't prove.
4. **Tell, don't list.** The tour speaks in short, evocative sentences; the cards carry the detail.

## Tone and look
- Moonlight, fog, old photographs in sepia that come to color when their ghost is active. Serif headlines, a calm spectral narrator.
- Spooky and wistful, never gory or disrespectful about loss.

## Non-goals
- Turn-by-turn walking navigation.
- User-submitted ghosts in v1.
